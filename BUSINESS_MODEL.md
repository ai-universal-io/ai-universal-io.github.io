# AI Universe — Business Model & Growth Playbook

## 1. What we are
**The fun, visual front door to the AI world.** Millions search "ChatGPT vs Claude" and "best AI for X" every month and land on boring blog listicles. We win by being *interactive*: quiz, battles, collectible cards, prompts. Users don't read us — they *play* us.

## 2. Growth loops (traffic engine)

### Loop 1 — The AI Card (viral, built ✅)
User makes a card with their name → posts on WhatsApp status / Instagram → friends ask "where's that from?" → they make theirs → repeat.
- Every card PNG is a free billboard. Add the site URL as a small footer on the card image before launch.
- "Collect all 10" brings the same user back repeatedly (each visit = another chance to share).
- Seasonal editions (Diwali gold card, New Year obsidian card) = recurring share spikes.

### Loop 2 — Quiz results (viral, built ✅)
"My perfect AI is Claude 🤖" is inherently shareable, like personality quizzes. Result share links carry the site URL.

### Loop 3 — SEO compounding (built ✅, needs deploy)
- Keywords: "ChatGPT vs Claude", "best free AI 2026", "best AI for coding", "[AI] prompts".
- Next: give each AI + each versus pair its own URL (`/claude`, `/chatgpt-vs-claude`) — 10 tools + 45 pairs = 55 indexable pages from data we already have.
- Structured data (done) targets Google rich results.

### Loop 4 — Retention hooks (built ✅)
Daily tip (24h rotation) + poll + favorites + prompt library = reasons to return. Newsletter converts anonymous visitors into an owned audience we can bring back on demand.

## 3. Revenue streams (in order of activation)

| # | Stream | How | When |
|---|--------|-----|------|
| 1 | **Affiliate / referral links** | Many AI tools pay for signups (Copilot via impact.com, hosting/tool affiliates, "Try X" buttons already exist — swap URLs for tracked links) | Day 1 |
| 2 | **Newsletter sponsorships** | AI newsletters sell $20–50 CPM. At 5k subs, one weekly sponsor slot ≈ $100–250/week | 1k+ subs |
| 3 | **Pro subscription $4.99/mo** | Prompt vault (500+), exclusive card skins, early model alerts, no ads. Waitlist is live on the site | 10k MAU |
| 4 | **Creator tier $19/mo** | White-label comparison widget + custom-branded cards for influencers' audiences | After Pro |
| 5 | **Sponsored placements** | "Featured AI of the week" slot, clearly labeled | 50k MAU |

**Philosophy:** traffic first, money second. Streams 1–2 are invisible to users; 3–5 only activate once we've earned trust.

## 4. KPIs to watch weekly
- **Traffic:** unique visitors, % from shares (UTM on card/quiz URLs)
- **Engagement:** cards created, quiz completions, prompts copied, avg session time (target 3+ min)
- **Retention:** returning-visitor %, newsletter signups (target 3–5% of visitors)
- **Revenue:** affiliate clicks → conversions, waitlist size

## 5. 90-day roadmap
**Days 1–14 — Launch**
- Deploy (Vercel free), real domain, Google Search Console + sitemap, Plausible analytics
- Wire newsletter to Beehiiv (free to 2.5k subs), add site URL footer onto card PNGs
- Launch posts: r/artificial, r/ChatGPT, Product Hunt, X, LinkedIn, 3 WhatsApp communities

**Days 15–45 — Content & SEO**
- Per-AI pages + versus pages (55 URLs), weekly "model news" blog post
- Swap Try-buttons to affiliate links where programs exist
- Hindi language toggle (huge underserved AI-comparison market in India)

**Days 46–90 — Monetize & deepen**
- Pro waitlist → founding-member launch at $2.99 locked-in
- Leaderboard: "Top card collectors this week" (name + card count)
- Seasonal card edition #1; first newsletter sponsor

## 6. Moat
Data + habit. The poll, quiz answers, and card collections tell us what real users prefer — that's proprietary insight no listicle has. The daily tip + collection mechanics build a check-in habit. Fast-moving AI news means our content refreshes weekly while old blog posts rot.
